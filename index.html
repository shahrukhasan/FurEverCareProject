Health Tips

Oral care

Check teeth and gums, use pet-safe dental products, and watch for pain or bad breath.

Healthy weight

Monitor body condition, portion sizes and activity. Sudden changes deserve attention.

Common warning signs

Notice changes in appetite, drinking, energy, breathing, skin, bathroom habits or behavior.

About FurEver Care

One portal, three pet-loving communities.

FurEver Care is a front-end demonstration portal designed to centralize everyday pet care information, pet product discovery, veterinarian information and shelter adoption content. It is intentionally serverless: display data comes from static JSON/TXT/XML files or browser session state.
